Cleanify

Flip through a playlist like a crate of records. Swipe right to keep a song, left to send it to the bargain bin.

Set up your Spotify app (once per browser)

Cleanify talks to Spotify through a free Spotify app of your own:

  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and press Create app.
  2. Give it any name and description. Under Redirect URIs, add and press Add.
  3. Tick Web API and Web Playback SDK, agree to the terms and press Save.
  4. Copy the app's Client ID, paste it here and press Save.

Friends can sign in to your app too: add their Spotify email under User Management (up to 5 people).

Your sign-in stays in this browser only.
